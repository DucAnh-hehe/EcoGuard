ECOGUARD IMAGE ASSET GUIDE
==========================

Put photos in this folder (assets/images/) using the EXACT filenames below.
Until a photo is added, every page shows a fallback instead (a team image
or a green/teal gradient), so nothing looks broken.

5) about-community-river.jpg
   Used on: about.html - top hero and the "Community" section background.
   Look for: people near a river, a clean-up activity or a riverside path.

Tips
- Landscape orientation, at least 1600 px wide, ideally under 400 KB (JPG).
- Avoid text, logos and watermarks. Pages place a dark overlay on heroes,
  so most photos stay readable.
- Suggested free sources: Unsplash or Pexels. Check the licence and any
  attribution requirement before publishing the site.
