Report Water Pollution

Help protect our rivers, lakes and waterways. If you notice unusual water colour, chemical discharge, foul odours or other signs of possible industrial wastewater pollution, let EcoGuard know.

Before you submit: Provide the location, date and clear photos if it is safe to do so. Do not touch or approach suspected contaminated water.
