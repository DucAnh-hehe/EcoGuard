Who we are and why we started

About EcoGuard

EcoGuard is a community-based platform designed to raise awareness and address industrial wastewater pollution in Hanoi, Vietnam.

Through our platform, residents can report suspected water pollution, share relevant information, explore reported locations and track the progress of their submissions.

We aim to connect local communities with environmental authorities and encourage everyone to take part in protecting our water resources.

Student prototype EcoGuard is currently a student project. Reports are saved only in your browser and are not sent to, or reviewed by, any authority.

💡

Our Inspirations

EcoGuard was inspired by growing concerns about industrial wastewater pollution and its impact on water quality, public health and the environment.

We believe that local communities play an important role in identifying environmental concerns. However, residents may not always have an accessible way to report pollution or communicate their concerns.

This inspired us to create a platform that gives people a voice and encourages collective action towards a cleaner and healthier environment.

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to make environmental reporting more accessible, transparent and community-driven.

We aim to:

  • Encourage residents to report suspected industrial wastewater pollution.
  • Raise awareness about water pollution and environmental protection.
  • Improve communication between local communities and environmental authorities.
  • Support a cleaner, safer and more sustainable water environment.

Understanding water pollution

Water Pollution Awareness

Industrial wastewater can contain harmful substances that negatively affect rivers, aquatic ecosystems and human health.

Understanding the causes and consequences of water pollution is an important step towards protecting our environment.

Common concerns include untreated wastewater discharge, chemical contamination and improper waste disposal.

💧

Water Quality Information

Water quality can be evaluated through different indicators that help determine the condition of a water source.

pH
Measures how acidic or alkaline the water is.
DO (Dissolved Oxygen)
Measures the amount of oxygen dissolved in water, which is important for aquatic life.
BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand)
Indicates the oxygen needed by microorganisms to break down organic matter.
COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand)
Measures the oxygen equivalent required to chemically oxidise substances in water.

These indicators help environmental specialists assess water quality and identify potential pollution problems.

Environmental Tips

Everyone can contribute to protecting water resources through small, everyday actions.

  • Avoid pouring chemicals, oils and harmful substances into drains.
  • Reduce the use of single-use plastics.
  • Dispose of household waste properly.
  • Participate in local river and waterway clean-up activities.
  • Report suspected water pollution through appropriate channels.
  • Share environmental knowledge with family and friends.

Questions and help

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is EcoGuard?

EcoGuard is a community-based platform that helps residents report suspected industrial wastewater pollution and access environmental information. It is currently a student prototype.

2. How can I report water pollution?

Visit the Report Pollution page, provide details about the suspected pollution and upload supporting photos if available.

3. Can I track my submitted report?

Yes. Visit the Track Reports page to check the progress and status of your report, if tracking is available for your submission. In this prototype, the status stages are a demonstration and reports are stored only in your browser.

4. Is EcoGuard free to use?

EcoGuard is designed as a free community platform to encourage public participation in environmental protection.

5. Does EcoGuard directly investigate pollution?

No. EcoGuard provides a channel for reporting suspected pollution. Relevant environmental authorities are responsible for verifying reports and conducting investigations. This prototype does not send reports to any authority.

6. Why is reporting water pollution important?

Reporting suspected pollution helps bring environmental concerns to the attention of relevant authorities and encourages community involvement.
